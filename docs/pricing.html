How crew billing works

Active crews determine the tier

Your plan follows the number of normal billable crews operating under the company.

Short overages get breathing room

One extra crew can be used for up to six rolling overage crew-days before an upgrade is required. Multiple extra crews consume multiple crew-days.

Storm crews are treated separately

Crews explicitly assigned to active Storm Mode are excluded from normal subscription crew counts while storm operations are active.
