Training is included with your LineCrew Pro subscription.

Every active subscriber gets access to a private, role-based Training Center. Videos are not published publicly and access follows each user’s company subscription and LineCrew Pro role.

Private by design

Training videos are stored privately and are available only after LineCrew Pro verifies the signed-in user, company subscription and role.

Active Subscribers Only

If a company subscription is suspended or cancelled, Training Center access is removed with the rest of subscriber access.

Role-Based Visibility

Foremen see Foreman training. General Foremen, Superintendents, Admins and Owners receive progressively broader training access.

Completion Tracking

Training progress and completion can be recorded so company leadership can confirm that users have completed their assigned material.

Support built into the product.

Training handles onboarding and repeat instruction, while LineCrew Pro support covers account and operational issues.

Admin AI Assistant

The Admin-only in-app assistant helps explain app features, troubleshoot common issues and guide company-level workflows.

Role-Based Video Library

The Training Center keeps Welcome, Foreman, General Foreman, Superintendent, Admin and Owner lessons organized in one private location.

Direct Support

Company leadership can contact LineCrew Pro support when an account, data or operational issue needs escalation.
