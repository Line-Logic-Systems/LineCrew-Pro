Role-based training

The training center is being built around short, task-focused lessons instead of one long generic manual.

Foreman Training

Morning JSA, selecting jobs and work points, entering installed and retired units, multiple poles, notes, signatures and submitting the daily report.

General Foreman Training

Reviewing production, approving daily reports, redlines, monitoring job progress and keeping crews aligned across active work.

Admin Training

Company setup, employees and roles, customers, contracts, price books, job packets, Storm Mode, approvals, reporting and administrative controls.
