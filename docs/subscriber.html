Install LineCrew Pro on your phone

LineCrew Pro is a progressive web app, so subscribers can add it to a phone or tablet home screen without using an app store.

  1. iPhone or iPad: Open the app in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: Open the app in Chrome, open the browser menu, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Finish: Open the new LineCrew Pro icon and sign in with your subscriber account.
