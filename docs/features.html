Product features

One operating system for field production, safety, timekeeping and oversight.

Built around the daily flow of distribution line contractors, from the Foreman in the field to the General Foreman and company Admin.

Daily Production Reports

Capture installed and retired units by job, work point and pole. Foremen can enter multiple locations and production lines without leaving the daily workflow.

Timekeeping

Track each crew member’s Regular and OT hours inside the same daily workflow. Admins can upload employee rosters, Foremen can maintain their normal crews, temporarily add employees from other crews, and export time records for payroll or billing systems.

Man-Hour Run Rate

Compare completed unit value against crew time with Regular and OT hours kept separate. OT is valued at 1.5× inside the run-rate calculation while the timekeeping record remains true to actual hours worked.

JSA & Crew Signatures

Keep the morning Job Safety Analysis separate from production reporting, with dedicated crew signatures and saved safety history.

Job Packets & Authorized Units

Import utility or customer job packets, map work points and authorized units, and reconcile field production against planned work.

GF Review & Approvals

General Foremen review daily reports, approve exceptions and handle redline units while maintaining a traceable history.

Contracts & Price Books

Maintain company-specific customer and utility pricing, including contract versions and unit adjustments for field visibility.

Storm Mode

Assign only selected crews to storm operations and keep storm production separate from normal work for cleaner tracking.

Job Progress

Track production against authorized job units and give supervisors and Admins a clearer view of percentage complete.

Role-Based Access

Foremen, General Foremen and Admins see the tools and pricing detail appropriate to their role.

Company Data Isolation & Security

Each contractor operates inside its own company account. Database access rules and role-based permissions are designed to keep one company’s jobs, pricing, production, employees, time records and reports from being visible to another.

LineCrew AI — Active

OpenAI-powered, Admin-only guidance answers naturally worded questions, remembers follow-up context and provides screen-by-screen help for LineCrew Pro workflows and troubleshooting. Authentication and company scoping are enforced before the assistant receives company context.

Your company data stays in your company.

Contractor accounts are separated by company, and access is scoped by both company membership and user role.

COMPANY SEPARATION

Tenant-isolated data

Jobs, contracts, price books, production, timekeeping, reports and employee records are tied to the contractor company that owns them.

ROLE SECURITY

Need-to-know access

Foreman, General Foreman and Admin permissions are separated so users do not automatically receive company-wide access.

PLATFORM CONTROL

Protected administration

Platform-level controls are separate from contractor accounts and reserved for explicitly authorized LineCrew Pro platform administration.
