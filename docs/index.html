Field work and office control in one system.

Replace disconnected spreadsheets, texts and paper workflows with company-specific operations that stay organized by job, work point, crew and day.

Daily Production

Foremen enter installed and retired units by job and work point, including multiple poles and production lines in a single day.

Timekeeping

Track every crew member’s Regular and OT hours, preload normal crews, add borrowed employees for the day, and export payroll- or billing-ready time records.

JSA & Safety

Dedicated morning Job Safety Analysis workflow with crew signatures and saved safety history separate from production reports.

Job Packets

Import utility job packets, work points and authorized units. Reconcile later packets against production already entered in the field.

GF Review & Redlines

General Foremen review daily reports, approve exceptions and manage redline units without losing the audit trail.

Pricing, Run Rate & Reporting

Maintain contract price books, track job progress, compare completed value to crew hours, and give the office the visibility needed to manage the business.

LineCrew AI

Active Admin-only AI guidance answers natural-language questions, remembers follow-up context and walks company Admins through LineCrew Pro workflows and troubleshooting.
