# Admin Timekeeping — Roster, Review & Export

## Goal
Teach an Admin how to upload an employee roster, review company time, filter records, and export payroll- or billing-ready files.

## Suggested runtime
3–4 minutes.

## Scene 1 — Open Timekeeping
**Callout:** Open Timekeeping

**Narration:** From the LineCrew Pro dashboard, open Timekeeping. Admins use this area to maintain the employee roster, review company time and prepare time records for payroll or billing systems.

## Scene 2 — Upload the employee roster
**Callout:** Upload Roster

**Narration:** In Employee Roster, choose an Excel or CSV file. The file must include an employee name column. Employee number, classification and crew columns are optional. LineCrew Pro detects the roster columns, previews how many employees are ready, and imports the roster into the contractor’s company account.

## Scene 3 — Review the roster
**Callout:** Review Employees

**Narration:** After import, review employee names, numbers, classifications and default crews. Employees can also be added individually. Foremen then use this company roster to select their normal crews, while permanent crew assignments remain protected from being claimed by another Foreman.

## Scene 4 — Run a time report
**Callout:** Run Time Report

**Narration:** Use the Time Report filters to choose a date range, employee or job. The summary shows employee count, total Regular hours, total OT hours and total actual hours for the selected records. The detailed table shows the employee, job, crew and storm status for each entry.

## Scene 5 — Export for payroll or billing
**Callout:** Export CSV

**Narration:** Choose Export CSV to create a payroll- or billing-ready file. The export includes employee number, employee name, classification, date, job number, job name, crew, Regular hours, OT hours, total hours and storm status. This lets the office move approved field time into its existing payroll, time or billing system without retyping the daily records.

## Scene 6 — Production run rate
**Callout:** Run Rate

**Narration:** The same crew time also feeds Production Reporting. Regular and OT stay separate in Timekeeping. For the man-hour run-rate calculation, Regular and OT hours are added together as total actual crew hours so the same work completed in the same time produces the same rate regardless of when the hours were worked.

## Closing
**Callout:** One Time Record

**Narration:** LineCrew Pro connects the roster, field time, daily production and office reporting. The Foreman enters time once, and the Admin can review and export it when payroll or billing is ready.
