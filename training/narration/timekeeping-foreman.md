# Foreman Timekeeping — My Crew & Daily Time

## Goal
Teach a Foreman how to set a normal crew, enter Regular and OT hours on a Daily Report, and temporarily add an employee from another crew without changing that employee’s permanent assignment.

## Suggested runtime
3–4 minutes.

## Scene 1 — Open Timekeeping
**Callout:** Open Timekeeping

**Narration:** From the LineCrew Pro dashboard, open Timekeeping. As a Foreman, this is where you manage the employees who normally work on your crew. Your normal crew is separate from the people who may help you for only one day.

## Scene 2 — Set My Crew
**Callout:** Set My Crew

**Narration:** In My Crew, select the employees who normally report to you and choose Save My Crew. Employees already assigned to another Foreman remain protected from permanent reassignment. Once your crew is saved, LineCrew Pro will automatically load those employees when you start a new Daily Report.

## Scene 3 — Start a Daily Report
**Callout:** Daily Crew Time

**Narration:** Open Production and start a Daily Report. Your normal crew appears in the Crew Time section automatically. For each employee, enter Regular hours and OT hours separately. LineCrew Pro totals the crew hours for the report, so there is no need to calculate a single crew total by hand.

## Scene 4 — Add a borrowed employee
**Callout:** Add Other Crew

**Narration:** If a lineman from another crew works with you for the day, choose Add Crew Member. You can select any active employee in the company roster. Employees assigned to another Foreman are labeled Other crew. Enter that employee’s Regular and OT hours for the day. This does not change their permanent crew assignment.

## Scene 5 — Save the report
**Callout:** Save Crew Time

**Narration:** Save the Daily Report and continue to production units. The time is stored with the employee, work date, job and crew where the employee actually worked. Those same hours feed company Timekeeping reports and production man-hour run-rate reporting.

## Closing
**Callout:** Time Entered Once

**Narration:** Enter each employee’s time once in the Daily Report. LineCrew Pro keeps Regular and OT hours separate, makes the records available to the office, and connects those hours to the work completed that day.
