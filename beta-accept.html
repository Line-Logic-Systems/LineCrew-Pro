Before you get started

LineCrew Pro requires two-step verification for Owner and Admin accounts. This protects company settings, employee information, billing and other sensitive business data.

You will need an authenticator app on your phone after you create your password.

  • Already have Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password or another TOTP authenticator? You are ready.
  • Don't have one yet? That's okay. Install one before continuing to the secure setup step.
  • LineCrew Pro will show you a QR code to scan, then you will enter the 6-digit code from your app.
